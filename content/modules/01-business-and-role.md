## What Revryze Does

Revryze is a pre-sales agency. We run 11–14 week pre-launch programs for boutique fitness, wellness, and service franchises. A client hires us to sell their first ~250 memberships before they open their doors — so they open full instead of empty.

Most franchise locations lose $20,000–$30,000 a month in their first six months because they open without a membership base. Revryze fulfills 90–95% of pre-launch membership targets. That's the value we deliver. That's why clients pay us a fixed fee per opening plus ad spend — and why they pay us again when they open a second location.

## The Economic Model (Why This Works)

- **Core fee per pre-launch:** $30,000–$40,000, paid to Revryze.
- **Ad spend:** Client also funds roughly $25,000 in ad spend (Meta), used to generate the lead pipeline you work.
- **Target:** ~250 founding memberships sold per location during the 11–14 week engagement.
- **Relaunch model:** for locations already open but underperforming, we sell in 50-member blocks at $6,000 per block — pay-as-you-go.

The economics force a tight team. Fewer reps with higher output means more dollars per rep. As we scale, new brands get added to the pipeline continuously, which is why this document is written to apply to every brand, not just one.

## Your Role

You are an Individual Contributor (IC) sales rep. That means:

- You **"own"** a book of business — the leads for those locations are yours to work, nurture, and close.
- You are expected to sell roughly **25 memberships per week** across your assigned locations.
- You work inside our CRM (GoHighLevel, also called GHL) plus each client's point-of-sale system (Hapana, Mindbody, or similar).

## Compensation

Compensation is commission-based with multiple accelerators. The exact dollar figures change as the company grows, so they are not printed here — your offer letter is the source of truth. The mechanics you need to understand:

- **Base commission:** paid on every membership you sell.
- **Fulfillment backpay:** when a location hits its target, every rep who contributed receives an additional 10% of their already-earned commission. This is the bonus that makes hitting the fulfillment number so valuable — in effect you earn roughly **300% of your running commission** on a successful engagement.
- **Speed-to-fulfillment bonus pool:** if a location hits its target ahead of the 12–14 week allotment, the unspent manpower cost becomes a rep bonus pool. Finish faster, earn more.
- **SPIFFs:** spot cash or bonuses tied to activity, not just sales. Examples: first to 25 sales in a week, first to book 10 calls in a day, first to hit 100 sales at a given location.
- **Relaunch commission pilot:** on 50-block relaunch accounts, we are testing 20% commission structures.

### Tax reality

Commission checks are taxed at a higher rate on the IRS's automatic schedule because withholding doesn't know your actual tax bracket. You often get some of it back at tax time. Consult a CPA for your own numbers; Revryze cannot give you tax advice.

## Career Path

Revryze is explicitly a two-track company:

- **IC track:** stay a full-time closer. Top ICs earn more than most sales managers.
- **Pod-leader track:** reps who excel in a vertical (fitness, medical, service) become pod leaders for that category. Pod leaders manage 3–5 reps, run training, and carry a scaled commission.

Ask your manager which track you're on. The training content is the same for both; the coaching expectations differ.

## The Culture

Three things matter most:

1. **Your leads are your book of business. Manage them or someone else will.** This is the #1 rule at Revryze. If you let a lead sit untouched, another rep will close that sale and earn your commission. Track every lead. Follow up within the hour. Mark everything read.
2. **We are nimble.** New brands get onboarded in weeks, not months. New tools get rolled out during the week. Processes change when a better one is found. If you need a process to be frozen to function, you will struggle here.
3. **Work-life balance is real.** You are expected to hit your numbers inside working hours, take your lunch, take your walks, and log off when the day is done. Shortening your break to grind harder is actively discouraged. Reps who burn out stop selling.

## North Star

> Revryze reps should be able to sell a tree to a lumberjack. That's the bar. You learn the product, you investigate the customer, you adapt in the moment, and you close. If that sounds like a craft instead of a script, that's because it is.
